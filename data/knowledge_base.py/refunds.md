# Refunds Policy

## 1. Refund Overview

A refund is the process of returning an eligible amount to the customer's original payment method or another approved method.

Refund eligibility and amount depend on:

- Order status
- Return or cancellation eligibility
- Reason for refund
- Product condition when applicable
- Applicable refund policy
- Payment method
- Seller or fulfillment conditions

The AI assistant must verify available order and refund information before confirming a refund.

---

## 2. When Is a Refund Available?

A refund may be available when:

- An eligible order is cancelled.
- An eligible product is returned.
- A return is approved and successfully processed.
- A product is unavailable and the applicable policy requires a refund.
- A payment was completed but the order could not be fulfilled.
- Another policy-approved refund condition applies.

The exact refund eligibility must be determined using the applicable order and refund policy.

---

## 3. Refund Request

To request assistance with a refund, the customer should provide:

- Order ID
- Product or item information when applicable
- Reason for the refund
- Relevant return information when applicable

The support system should verify the order before confirming the refund status.

---

## 4. Refund Process

A typical refund process may include:

1. Customer requests a refund or becomes eligible for a refund.
2. Order and refund eligibility are verified.
3. Return or cancellation requirements are completed when applicable.
4. Refund is approved.
5. Refund is initiated.
6. Payment provider processes the transaction.
7. Amount is credited according to the payment method's processing time.
8. Refund is marked completed when confirmation is available.

The exact process can vary by order and payment method.

---

## 5. Refund Status

### Refund Requested

The customer has requested a refund and the request is awaiting verification or processing.

### Refund Under Review

The refund request is being checked against the applicable order and policy.

### Refund Approved

The refund has been approved and is ready for processing or has entered the refund workflow.

### Refund Processing

The refund has been initiated or is currently being processed.

### Refund Completed

The refund has been successfully processed according to the available payment or refund system.

### Refund Failed

The refund could not be completed successfully.

The customer should contact support if the system reports a failed refund.

### Refund Rejected

The refund request was not approved based on the applicable eligibility conditions.

---

## 6. How Long Does a Refund Take?

Refund timing depends on:

- Refund processing by the store
- Payment method
- Payment provider
- Banking or financial institution
- Weekends and holidays
- Additional verification requirements

The AI must not provide a guaranteed refund date unless the underlying system explicitly provides one.

When available, an order-specific refund estimate should take priority over general information.

---

## 7. Refund Not Received

If a customer has not received an expected refund:

1. Verify the order ID.
2. Check the current refund status.
3. Confirm whether the refund has been initiated.
4. Check the expected processing period.
5. Consider the payment method's processing time.
6. Contact support if the expected processing period has passed.

The AI should not claim that a refund has been completed unless the refund system confirms it.

---

## 8. Refund Processing but Amount Not Received

If the refund status shows "Processing":

The refund may still be moving through the payment provider or banking system.

The customer should wait for the applicable processing period before escalating the issue unless the system indicates a problem.

---

## 9. Refund Marked Completed but Not Received

If the refund system reports that a refund is completed but the customer cannot see the amount:

1. Confirm the refund status.
2. Confirm the refund amount.
3. Verify the original payment method.
4. Check the customer's bank or payment-provider statement.
5. Contact support if the amount remains unavailable after the applicable processing period.

Support may need to provide the available refund reference or transaction information.

---

## 10. Refund Failed

A refund can fail because of:

- Payment-method issues
- Payment-provider errors
- Bank processing issues
- Invalid or unavailable refund destination
- Technical problems
- Transaction restrictions

If a refund fails, the case should be reviewed by the appropriate support team.

The AI must not promise that a failed refund will automatically be retried unless the system confirms this.

---

## 11. Refund Rejected

A refund may be rejected when:

- The order is not eligible.
- The return requirements were not met.
- The applicable return period has expired.
- The product does not satisfy the applicable policy.
- The requested amount is not supported by the order.
- Another policy condition prevents the refund.

The customer should receive an explanation based on the actual order and policy information.

---

## 12. Full Refund

A full refund means the eligible refundable amount for the relevant order or item is returned.

The exact amount may depend on:

- Product price
- Discounts
- Taxes
- Shipping charges
- Return shipping charges
- Other applicable adjustments

The AI must not assume that every refund is a full refund.

---

## 13. Partial Refund

A partial refund means only part of the original payment is refunded.

Partial refunds may occur because of:

- Partial order cancellation
- Partial return
- Missing or damaged items
- Applicable shipping charges
- Discounts or promotions
- Other policy-based adjustments

The refund amount should be confirmed using the actual order and refund information.

---

## 14. Multiple Items in One Order

If an order contains multiple products, one or more items may be refunded separately.

Each item may have:

- Different return status
- Different refund amount
- Different refund eligibility
- Different processing status

The support system should verify item-level information when available.

---

## 15. Refund for Cancelled Order

If an eligible order is cancelled after payment:

1. Verify the cancellation status.
2. Verify refund eligibility.
3. Confirm the refundable amount.
4. Initiate the refund when authorized.
5. Provide the refund status to the customer.

The AI must not claim that a refund has been initiated unless the refund system confirms the action.

---

## 16. Refund After Return

For returned products:

1. Return eligibility is verified.
2. Return is approved.
3. Product is returned.
4. Returned product may be inspected.
5. Refund eligibility and amount are confirmed.
6. Refund is initiated.
7. Payment provider processes the refund.

The refund should not be represented as completed until the applicable system confirms completion.

---

## 17. Refund to Original Payment Method

When supported, refunds are normally returned to the original payment method.

The exact processing time depends on the payment provider and financial institution.

The customer should verify the original payment account or payment instrument when checking for the refund.

---

## 18. Payment Method Unavailable

If the original payment method is no longer available, the customer should contact support.

Alternative refund procedures may depend on:

- Payment provider rules
- Store policy
- Transaction status
- Verification requirements

The AI must not promise an alternative refund method without confirmation.

---

## 19. Card Payment Refund

For card payments, the refund may require additional processing time after it is initiated.

The customer should check the relevant card account or statement.

If the refund remains unavailable beyond the expected processing period, contact support.

---

## 20. Digital Payment Refund

For digital or online payment methods, refund timing depends on the payment provider.

The customer should check the relevant payment account after the refund is marked as processed.

If the amount does not appear within the expected period, support can investigate the transaction.

---

## 21. Bank Transfer Refund

Bank-transfer refunds may require additional banking processing time.

Customers should verify the relevant bank account and contact support if the refund does not appear after the applicable processing period.

---

## 22. Cash-on-Delivery Orders

Refund handling for cash-on-delivery orders depends on the store's supported refund methods and applicable policy.

The customer may need to provide additional payment information or complete verification.

The AI should not request sensitive banking credentials.

---

## 23. Refund for Shipping Charges

Whether shipping charges are refundable depends on the applicable refund policy and reason for the refund.

The AI must not assume that shipping charges are always refundable.

---

## 24. Discount and Promotional Orders

Refund calculations for discounted or promotional orders may depend on:

- Discount type
- Coupon conditions
- Items returned
- Items cancelled
- Promotion rules

The final refund amount should be calculated by the applicable order or refund system.

---

## 25. Refund Reference

When available, the support system may provide a refund reference or transaction identifier.

This information can help the customer or support team track the refund with the relevant payment provider.

Only verified refund references should be provided.

---

## 26. Duplicate Refund

If a customer believes they received more than one refund for the same transaction:

1. Verify the order.
2. Check the refund transaction history.
3. Identify duplicate transactions if present.
4. Escalate the issue to the appropriate payment or finance team.

The AI should not make assumptions about duplicate transactions without transaction data.

---

## 27. Incorrect Refund Amount

If the customer believes the refund amount is incorrect:

1. Verify the order ID.
2. Check the original order amount.
3. Check returned or cancelled items.
4. Check discounts and applicable adjustments.
5. Check the refund transaction.
6. Escalate the case when necessary.

The AI should explain only amounts confirmed by the underlying system.

---

## 28. Refund Investigation

A refund may require additional investigation when:

- Refund status is inconsistent.
- Refund amount is disputed.
- Refund failed.
- Refund was marked completed but cannot be located.
- Multiple refunds appear for the same order.
- The original payment method is unavailable.
- The customer disputes the refund decision.

The case should be escalated to human support when automated verification is insufficient.

---

## 29. Refund Information Required

When handling a refund-related request, the support system may need:

- Customer ID
- Order ID
- Item ID
- Refund ID
- Payment method
- Refund status
- Refund amount
- Transaction reference

Only information necessary to investigate the refund should be requested.

Never request:

- Passwords
- OTPs
- PINs
- CVVs
- Full payment-card credentials
- API keys
- Authentication tokens

---

## 30. AI Support Rules for Refunds

The AI customer-support assistant must follow these rules:

1. Never invent refund amounts.
2. Never invent refund dates.
3. Never claim a refund was initiated without system confirmation.
4. Never claim a refund was completed without system confirmation.
5. Never guarantee a refund before eligibility is verified.
6. Use order-specific refund information whenever available.
7. Distinguish clearly between requested, approved, processing, completed, failed, and rejected refunds.
8. Explain that payment-provider processing time may differ from store processing time.
9. Escalate failed, disputed, or unusual refund cases when necessary.
10. Never expose internal payment information.
11. Never request passwords, OTPs, PINs, CVVs, or authentication credentials.
12. Provide only refund references and transaction information confirmed by the underlying system.
