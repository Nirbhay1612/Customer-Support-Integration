# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. General Information

### What are your customer support hours?

Our customer support team is available during regular business hours. Customers can contact support through the available support channels for assistance with orders, payments, returns, refunds, and other issues.

### How can I contact customer support?

You can contact customer support through the support channel provided by the store. For issues that require human assistance, a support ticket can be created and assigned to the appropriate support team.

### How can I check my order?

You can check your order status using your order ID. The support system can retrieve available order information and provide the current order status.

### What information should I provide when contacting support?

For faster assistance, provide relevant information such as:

- Customer ID or registered email address
- Order ID
- Description of the issue
- Relevant payment or delivery information when applicable

Never share passwords, authentication codes, API keys, or other security credentials with customer support.

## 2. Account

### How can I update my account information?

Customers can request an update to supported account information through the available account-management or customer-support channels.

### I cannot access my account. What should I do?

First, use the account recovery process provided by the store. If you still cannot access the account, contact customer support for further assistance.

Do not share your password or one-time authentication codes with support.

### How can I change my email address?

If email changes are supported, update the email address through the account settings. If the option is unavailable, contact customer support and complete any required identity verification.

---

## 3. Orders

### How do I place an order?

Select the products you want, add them to your cart, provide the required delivery information, select an available payment method, and complete checkout.

### Where can I find my order ID?

The order ID is normally shown on the order confirmation page and in the order confirmation message or email.

### What does "Order Pending" mean?

"Pending" generally means that the order has been received but has not yet completed the next processing stage.

### What does "Order Processing" mean?

"Processing" means that the order is being prepared for fulfillment. The order may move to shipment once processing is completed.

### What does "Shipped" mean?

"Shipped" means that the order has been handed over for delivery or has entered the shipping process.

### What does "Delivered" mean?

"Delivered" means that the delivery system reports the order as delivered.

If you did not receive an order marked as delivered, contact customer support and provide the order ID.

### Can I cancel my order?

Order cancellation depends on the current order status and the store's cancellation policy.

If cancellation is available, the request should be validated before the cancellation action is performed.

### Can I change my order after placing it?

Changes may depend on whether the order has entered processing or shipping. Contact support as soon as possible if you need to change an order.

---

## 4. Shipping

### How long does shipping take?

Shipping time depends on the selected delivery method, destination, product availability, and carrier.

The actual delivery estimate should be checked against the customer's order information when available.

### How can I track my shipment?

Provide your order ID to customer support. If tracking information is available, the support system can provide the tracking number and current shipment status.

### My tracking information has not updated. What should I do?

Tracking updates can sometimes take time to appear after a shipment is handed to the carrier.

If tracking has not updated for an extended period, contact customer support with your order ID.

### My order is delayed. What should I do?

Check the latest order and tracking information first. If the order is significantly delayed, contact customer support for further assistance.

### What should I do if my package is marked delivered but I did not receive it?

Verify the delivery address and check whether the package was left in a safe location or received by another person at the delivery address.

If the package is still missing, contact customer support and provide the order ID.

---

## 5. Returns

### Can I return a product?

Returns depend on the product, order status, applicable return policy, and return eligibility requirements.

Check the store's return policy or contact customer support before sending an item back.

### How do I request a return?

Provide your order ID and explain the reason for the return. Customer support can verify whether the order is eligible and provide the next steps.

### What information is required for a return request?

A return request may require:

- Order ID
- Product information
- Reason for return
- Photos or other evidence when applicable
- Additional information required by the return policy

### Can I return a damaged product?

Damaged-product returns may be eligible depending on the store's return policy.

Report the issue as soon as possible and provide the order ID and any requested evidence.

### Can I return the wrong product I received?

If you received an incorrect product, contact customer support with your order ID and product details so the issue can be investigated.

---

## 6. Refunds

### When will I receive my refund?

Refund processing time depends on the store's refund process and the payment provider or financial institution.

Once a refund is processed, the time required for the amount to appear can vary by payment method.

### How can I check my refund status?

Provide the relevant order ID or refund information to customer support. The support team can check the available refund status.

### My refund has not arrived. What should I do?

First confirm whether the refund has been processed.

If the refund was processed but has not appeared within the expected processing period, contact customer support with the order ID and refund details.

### Will I receive a full refund?

The refund amount depends on the applicable refund policy, order details, returned items, shipping charges, discounts, and other applicable conditions.

The support team should verify the specific order before confirming the refund amount.

## 7. Payments

### What payment methods are supported?

Available payment methods depend on the store's configuration and checkout options.

Customers should use the payment methods displayed during checkout.

### My payment failed. What should I do?

Check that the payment information is correct and that your payment method is active.

If the problem continues, try another available payment method or contact your payment provider.

### I was charged but my order was not created. What should I do?

Contact customer support with the relevant order or transaction information so the payment can be investigated.

Never share your password, PIN, CVV, or one-time authentication code with support.

### Can I change my payment method after placing an order?

Payment-method changes depend on the order status and store policies. Contact support if you need assistance.

## 14. Support Response Guidelines

The AI support assistant should follow these rules when answering customer questions:

1. Use the knowledge base as the primary source for general policy information.
2. Do not invent policies, prices, delivery dates, refund amounts, or order information.
3. Use real order/customer data when available instead of guessing.
4. Ask for an order ID when order-specific information is required.
5. Do not expose internal system information.
6. Never request passwords, API keys, OTPs, PINs, or authentication credentials.
7. Do not claim that an action was completed unless the corresponding system confirms it.
8. Escalate issues that require human investigation.
9. Keep responses clear, concise, and relevant to the customer's question.
10. When information is unavailable, clearly state that the information could not be verified.
