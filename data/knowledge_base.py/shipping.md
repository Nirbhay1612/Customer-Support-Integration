# Shipping Policy

## 1. Shipping Overview

We aim to process and deliver customer orders efficiently.

Shipping availability, delivery time, and delivery charges may depend on:

- Customer location
- Product availability
- Selected shipping method
- Order processing time
- Carrier availability
- Holidays and non-working days
- Weather or other unexpected events

The delivery estimate shown for a specific order should be treated as the primary reference when available.

---

## 2. Order Processing

After an order is successfully placed, it may go through the following stages:

1. Order received
2. Payment verification
3. Order processing
4. Packing
5. Shipment handover
6. In transit
7. Out for delivery
8. Delivered

An order may take additional time to process before it is handed over to the delivery carrier.

---

## 3. Shipping Time

Delivery time depends on the destination, shipping method, product availability, and carrier.

Customers should check the delivery estimate associated with their specific order whenever available.

General shipping estimates should not be treated as guaranteed delivery dates.

If an order exceeds the expected delivery period, the customer can contact support with the order ID.

---

## 4. Shipping Status

### Pending

The order has been received but has not yet progressed to the next processing stage.

### Processing

The order is being prepared for fulfillment.

### Packed

The product has been prepared for shipment.

### Shipped

The order has been handed over to the delivery carrier or has entered the shipping process.

### In Transit

The shipment is moving through the carrier network toward the delivery destination.

### Out for Delivery

The shipment has reached the local delivery stage and may be delivered soon.

### Delivered

The carrier reports that the shipment has been delivered.

### Delivery Failed

The carrier was unable to complete delivery.

The customer may need to follow the carrier's instructions or contact support for further assistance.

---

## 5. Tracking Orders

Customers can request shipment tracking information by providing their order ID.

When tracking information is available, support may provide:

- Tracking number
- Shipment status
- Latest tracking update
- Available delivery estimate

The support system should only provide tracking information returned by the configured order or shipping system.

The AI must not invent tracking numbers or delivery statuses.

---

## 6. Tracking Number Not Available

A tracking number may not be available immediately after an order is placed.

If an order has not yet been shipped, tracking information may be unavailable.

Customers should check again after the order enters the shipping process.

---

## 7. Tracking Information Not Updating

Tracking information may take time to update after a package is handed to the carrier.

If tracking has not changed for an extended period:

1. Verify the order ID.
2. Check the latest available tracking status.
3. Confirm whether the shipment is still within the expected delivery period.
4. Contact support if the delay appears unusual.

Do not assume that a missing tracking update means the package is lost.

---

## 8. Delayed Orders

An order may be delayed because of:

- Carrier delays
- High shipping volume
- Incorrect or incomplete address information
- Weather conditions
- Operational issues
- Product availability
- Holidays
- Unexpected transportation disruptions

When a customer reports a delayed order, support should first check the latest order and tracking information.

---

## 9. Order Marked Delivered but Not Received

If an order is marked as delivered but the customer has not received it:

1. Confirm the delivery address.
2. Check around the delivery location.
3. Check whether another household member or authorized person received the package.
4. Check any available delivery notes or proof of delivery.
5. Contact customer support if the package cannot be located.

The support team may need to investigate the delivery with the carrier.

---

## 10. Incorrect Delivery Address

Customers should verify their delivery address before completing checkout.

If an incorrect address was entered, contact support as soon as possible.

Address changes may not be possible after an order has entered processing or shipping.

The support team must verify the current order status before confirming whether an address change is possible.

---

## 11. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges may depend on:

- Delivery location
- Shipping method
- Order value
- Product type
- Seller or fulfillment method
- Promotional offers

The final shipping charge shown during checkout should be treated as the applicable charge for the order.

The AI should not invent shipping charges.

---

## 12. Free Shipping

Free shipping may be available for eligible orders depending on the store's current shipping policy or promotional offers.

Eligibility requirements may include:

- Minimum order value
- Selected products
- Delivery location
- Promotional conditions

Customers should check the applicable offer or checkout information.

---

## 13. Multiple Products in One Order

Products from the same order may sometimes be shipped together or separately.

Separate shipments may have different:

- Tracking numbers
- Shipping statuses
- Delivery dates

The support system should use the actual shipment information available for the order.

---

## 14. Partial Delivery

If only part of an order has been delivered:

1. Check whether the order was split into multiple shipments.
2. Review available tracking information.
3. Check the status of the remaining items.
4. Contact support if the remaining items are outside the expected delivery period.

---

## 15. Damaged Package

If a package arrives visibly damaged:

- Check the condition of the product.
- Keep the packaging when possible.
- Take relevant photographs if requested.
- Contact support with the order ID.
- Follow the return or replacement process provided by support.

Do not dispose of potentially relevant packaging before the issue is resolved if the support process requires it.

---

## 16. Wrong Product Delivered

If the customer receives a product different from the ordered product:

1. Verify the order details.
2. Do not use the product if instructed by the return policy.
3. Contact support with the order ID.
4. Provide product details and requested evidence.
5. Follow the return or replacement instructions.

---

## 17. Delivery Attempt Failed

If delivery was unsuccessful, the carrier may attempt delivery again depending on its procedures.

Customers should check the latest tracking information.

If repeated delivery attempts fail, contact customer support for assistance.

---

## 18. Customer Not Available

If the customer is unavailable during delivery, the carrier may follow its standard re-delivery or delivery-attempt process.

The exact process depends on the carrier and destination.

Customers should check the latest tracking information for available instructions.

---

## 19. International Shipping

International shipping availability depends on the store's supported destinations and product restrictions.

International deliveries may involve:

- Longer delivery times
- Customs processing
- Import requirements
- Additional charges
- Carrier-specific procedures

Customers should check the applicable international shipping information before placing an order.

---

## 20. Customs and Import Delays

International shipments may be delayed by customs processing.

Possible factors include:

- Customs inspection
- Import documentation
- Duties or taxes
- Local regulations
- Carrier processing

The support system should not guarantee a customs clearance date.

---

## 21. Shipping Restrictions

Some products or destinations may have shipping restrictions.

Restrictions can depend on:

- Product type
- Destination
- Local regulations
- Carrier policies
- Seller requirements

If a product cannot be shipped to a particular location, the customer should be informed through the applicable checkout or support process.

---

## 22. Changing Shipping Method

Shipping-method changes may only be possible before an order reaches a certain processing stage.

Customers should contact support as soon as possible.

The support system must verify the current order status before confirming a shipping-method change.

---

## 23. Cancelling a Shipped Order

An order that has already entered the shipping process may not be eligible for direct cancellation.

Depending on the applicable policy, the customer may need to:

1. Wait for delivery.
2. Follow the return process.
3. Contact customer support for available options.

The AI should never promise cancellation without verifying the order status.

---

## 24. Delivery Estimate

A delivery estimate is an expected timeframe rather than a guaranteed delivery time unless the applicable service explicitly provides a guarantee.

When available, the order-specific delivery estimate should take priority over general shipping information.

---

## 25. Shipping Support Information

When handling a shipping-related customer request, collect the following information when necessary:

- Customer ID
- Order ID
- Tracking number
- Current order status
- Customer location
- Description of the shipping issue

The support system should request only the information necessary to resolve the issue.

Never request:

- Passwords
- OTPs
- PINs
- CVVs
- API keys
- Authentication tokens

---

## 26. AI Support Rules for Shipping

The AI customer-support assistant must follow these rules:

1. Never invent tracking information.
2. Never invent delivery dates.
3. Never guarantee delivery unless the underlying system explicitly provides a guarantee.
4. Use order-specific information whenever available.
5. Ask for the order ID when order-specific information is required.
6. Clearly distinguish between an estimate and a confirmed status.
7. Do not claim an order was shipped unless the order system confirms it.
8. Do not claim an order was delivered unless the available tracking information confirms it.
9. Escalate unusual or unresolved delivery issues when necessary.
10. Never request passwords, OTPs, PINs, CVVs, or authentication credentials.
